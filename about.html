Our story

Good design is more than how a room looks.

Nova Interior was founded on a simple idea: that a well-designed space should feel effortless to live in. We bring your ideas together with practical planning, natural materials and a thoughtful eye for the details that make everyday life feel special.

From the first sketch to the finishing touches, our approach begins with listening — so the result feels personal, purposeful and distinctly yours.

What we believe

Design principles we come back to.

Every project is different, but the values behind the way we work stay the same.

01 / LISTEN

Understanding first

We begin by getting to know you — your routine, your taste, your space and the way you want to feel in it.

02 / PLAN

Thoughtful planning

Good interiors start with good layouts. We prioritise flow, function and comfort before finishes and furniture.

03 / MATERIALS

Material-led detail

Natural textures, honest finishes and considered palettes bring warmth and character to every space.

04 / CLARITY

Clear communication

We keep the process transparent — from the first conversation to the final handover — so you always know what’s next.

05 / CRAFT

Attention to detail

The finishing touches are what make a space feel finished. We sweat the small stuff so you don’t have to.

06 / CARE

Designed for living

We design for real life, not just photographs — spaces that work beautifully today and feel right for years to come.
