Common questions

Good to know before we start.

A few answers to the questions we hear most often. Not seeing yours? Get in touch — we’re happy to help.

How does the design process begin?
It starts with a conversation. We’ll talk about your space, your goals, your style and your budget — then recommend the level of support that fits. From there, we agree the scope and timeline before any design work begins.
How long does a typical project take?
It depends on scope. A single-space concept might take a few weeks, while a full home or commercial project can take several months. We’ll give you a clear timeline during the consultation and keep you updated throughout.
Can I hire you for just a floor plan or 3D visual?
Yes. Our services are flexible — you can book a single service like a floor plan or 3D visualisation, or combine several into a full design package. Many clients start small and expand later.
Do you work outside your city?
We work on both local and remote projects. Many design services — plans, visualisations, styling direction — can be delivered remotely with video calls. Add your service area details here before launch.
What do you need from me to get started?
A few things help: basic measurements or a floor plan (if you have one), photos of the space, a sense of your style and budget, and any timelines you’re working to. Don’t worry if you don’t have all of it — we can help fill in the gaps.
How is pricing worked out?
Pricing depends on scope, space size and the level of support you need. After the initial consultation, we provide a clear written proposal covering the deliverables, timeline and cost — with no surprises.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell us about your space — we’ll help you work out the right starting point.
