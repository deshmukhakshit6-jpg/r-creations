Listen & Discover

Every project begins with a conversation. We take the time to understand how you live, what you love, and what you want your space to do for you — before any design decisions are made.

Common questions

Questions about the process.

Not seeing your question? Get in touch — we’re happy to help.

Do I need to have everything decided before we start?
Not at all. The discovery conversation is exactly where we figure things out together. You don’t need to have your style, budget or layout pinned down — we’ll help you explore options and make confident decisions.
Can we go faster if I’m on a tight timeline?
Often, yes. If you have a deadline, tell us early so we can scope the project accordingly and agree which stages can be compressed. Faster timelines may affect the scope, so we’ll be upfront about trade-offs.
What happens if I want to change the design mid-way?
Changes are part of the process — that’s what the refine stage is for. We build in a round of revisions and will discuss any changes that go beyond the agreed scope, so there are no surprises.
Do you manage the build or just the design?
Design is our core service. Depending on scope, we can coordinate with contractors and suppliers, or hand over documentation so you or your builder can proceed. We’ll clarify what’s included in your proposal.
How do we stay in touch during the project?
Communication is part of the service. We agree on a rhythm — check-ins, milestones and review points — so you always know what’s happening and what’s next. Email, calls and scheduled reviews keep everything on track.
What do I need to prepare before our first call?
A few things help: photos of your space, any measurements or plans you already have, a sense of your style and budget, and any timelines you’re working to. Don’t worry if you don’t have all of it — we can help fill in the gaps.
