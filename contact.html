Common questions

Before you get in touch.

Quick answers to the questions we hear most often. Not seeing yours? Send us a message.

What happens after I send the form?
We’ll read your enquiry and get back to you — usually within a couple of working days — to arrange a short conversation about your space, goals, timeline and budget. From there, we’ll recommend the right next step.
Is the first consultation free?
Our initial conversation is about understanding your project — we’ll let you know clearly what’s included and any costs before you commit to anything. Add your specific policy here before launch.
Can I share photos or plans with my enquiry?
Yes, absolutely — photos or plans help a lot. Mention in your message that you have them and we’ll reply with a way to send them securely.
Do you work remotely or only locally?
We work on both local and remote projects. Many services — plans, visualisations, styling direction — can be delivered remotely with video calls. Add your service area details here before launch.
How soon can we start?
That depends on scope and our current schedule. Tell us your timeline in the form and we’ll be upfront about availability and what’s realistic.
